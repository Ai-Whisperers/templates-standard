# VENDOR POWER
This metric definition is important due to strategy and competition practices that we could overcome by understanding the modern financial and business paradigm.

## Power - Definition in the "Vendors" paradigm
Measure of the capabilities of a particular vendors in terms of "execution". Execution could be a strategic move, compliance move or any action that these vendors could perform in order to:

- Reduce competitors power (Always in the most beneficial ways, never an aggression or law-enforcing strategy )
- Reduce new competitors (Both in spawn number and power potential)

- Ecosystem: maturity, engagement/popularity and whether is a more dev/power-user centered or more for normal final-users.
- Lock-in aggresivity
